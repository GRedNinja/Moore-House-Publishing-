Logic a Brief TextBook

First published in 1891, Charles Coppens’s A Brief Text‑Book of Logic and Mental Philosophy offered students a disciplined way of seeing: how we know, and how we test the certainty of what we know. This edition preserves Coppens’s clarity while adding practical exercises that draw the reader into the work of discernment, sifting truth from error in the tangled currents of social media, public commentary, and daily life.
